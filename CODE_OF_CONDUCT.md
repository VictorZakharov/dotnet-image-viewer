# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss ideas and code rather than
attacking people. Assume good intent, accept correction gracefully, and make
room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's
private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Contact the maintainer
privately through the contact options on the
[VictorZakharov GitHub profile](https://github.com/VictorZakharov). Include links
or screenshots needed to understand the incident.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports
will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a
reporter or participant in an investigation is itself a violation.
